🧠 Radiation Awareness Center

Understand radiation, where it comes from, when exposure can be safe, possible effects, and practical ways to reduce unnecessary exposure.

☢️ What is Radiation?

Radiation is energy that travels as waves or particles. It is part of everyday life and can be natural or produced by people.

⚖️ Risk depends on exposure

For ionizing radiation, risk depends on factors such as radiation type, dose, dose rate, and how long or how often exposure occurs.

📊 Read charts carefully

A chart is meaningful only when its unit, measurement period and data source are known. Demo visuals should not be treated as real measurements.

Is radiation safe?

Radiation is not automatically dangerous. Safety depends on the type, amount of exposure and circumstances. Many useful applications are performed under controlled conditions.

✅ Lower-risk situations

Everyday background radiation and properly controlled medical or workplace uses can be managed using established safety practices.

⚠️ When concern increases

Higher or unnecessary exposure, particularly to ionizing radiation, can increase health risks. Avoid unnecessary exposure and follow safety guidance.

🌍 Where does radiation come from?

Radiation has both natural and human-made source. The source alone does not determine risk; exposure conditions matter.

🌎 Natural

Cosmic radiation and naturally occurring radioactive materials in soil, rocks, water and the air.

🏥 Medical

X-rays, CT scans and some nuclear medicine procedures use radiation for diagnosis or treatment.

🏭 Industrial

Some industries use radiation sources for inspection, measurement, research and other controlled applications.

📱 Everyday technology

Wireless devices and communication systems use non-ionizing radiofrequency electromagnetic fields.

Important: The awareness categories above describe sources, not measured percentages of exposure for a particular state.

👨 Humans

High doses of ionizing radiation can cause tissue injury. Lower doses may increase long-term health risk, depending on exposure.

🐾 Animals

Significant radiation exposure can affect biological processes, development and reproduction in animals.

🌱 Plants

High radiation exposure can affect plant growth and cellular or biological processes.

📌 Remember

Effects cannot be judged from a radiation label alone. The radiation type, dose and exposure conditions must be considered.

🛡️ Precautions to Stay Safe

⏱️ Reduce Exposure Time

Minimize unnecessary time near radiation sources.

📏 Increase Distance

Maintain a safe distance from radiation sources.

🛡️ Use Shielding

Use appropriate shielding and protective equipment when required.

📋 Follow Safety Rules

Always follow official radiation safety instructions.
